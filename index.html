2.2 kW GaN AC–DC charger ChargeWorks Developed a GaN charger, evaluated DAB and LLC topologies, and optimized the resonant tank and magnetics.
  • Developed a GaN charger combining Totem-Pole PFC with an optimized LLC converter and EPC GaN synchronous rectification on the secondary side.
  • Compared DAB and LLC architectures for efficiency, current stress, operating characteristics, and implementation tradeoffs, selecting LLC for the charger.
  • Developed Triple Phase Shift modulation for DAB and formulated an RMS-current minimization problem to reduce conduction losses and current stress.
  • Optimized resonant and magnetizing inductance, tank capacitance, transformer design, switching-frequency range, and device stress.
  • Designed schematics, PCB layouts, magnetic components, GaN gate drives, and digital control strategies, supported by simulation and hardware testing.
  • Measured best-case stage efficiencies of approximately 97.3% for the bridgeless Totem-Pole PFC stage and 98.8% for the LLC stage.
